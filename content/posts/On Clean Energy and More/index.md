If you haven't yet you need to watch this video regardless of your feelings on the subject of solar power or renewables in general, its full of good information. I know that it is a long video but if you keep reading I will offer a suggestion to shorten it.

If you aren't familiar with what this video is or what it is about I will give you a primer. This guy Alec makes videos about various technologies. In this particular video he speaks on several topics dear to me. The first of which is that the world is ready to switch to getting ALL of its electricity from means other than burning things. At, functionally a "moments" notice we could just decided to stop burning coal and methane to generate electricity. Why don't we? Because it is more profitable for oil companies if we just keep needing to buy things that burn, instead of spending a fraction of that same money upfront to "pre-purchase" enough solar generation to power the same things over their lifetime.

Well, that's the first 2/3 of the video. The last 3rd is...a different, and more important video. If you only have 30min, please, do me a favor skip to the [1h03m](https://youtu.be/KtQ9nt2ZeGM?si=Y5601GXQABL5XcYR&t=3760) mark and click play. Watch from that point forward with an open mind, an open heart, and a modicum of introspection. IF you do that, I want to say thank you. I appreciate you.
